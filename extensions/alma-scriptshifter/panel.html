ScriptShifter for Alma

Click a text field in Alma, then capture it here. This panel stays open while you switch fields.

For a parallel value, first create the alternate field/property using Alma, click its empty text control, then insert here. The extension does not create MARC 880 links or BIBFRAME properties. Conversion sends text to bibframe.org; Alma credentials are never sent.
